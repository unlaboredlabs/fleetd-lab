fleetd's merge train is the component that lands pull requests on `main` in this repo, and writers never merge their own work. After a writer opens a pull request, fleetd spawns the tier reviews that its risk level requires. Once those reviews pass, the train rebases the branch onto the current `main` and waits for the required `ci` check to go green before merging. The train is the only actor that merges, and it is also the only actor that posts the `fleetd/train` commit status that `main` requires. If the rebase conflicts or `ci` fails, the train does not merge; it returns the problem to the writer so the branch can be fixed and requeued.
