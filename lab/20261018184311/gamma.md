fleetd's merge train is the step that actually merges pull requests into `main`. A pull request enters the train only after its tier reviews have passed; the train then rebases it onto the current `main`, waits for the CI checks to go green on the rebased commit, and merges it. Writers never merge: agents that produce changes open pull requests and stop there, and the train is the only merger. Because the train also posts the `fleetd/train` commit status, a pull request that the train has not processed does not carry that status.
